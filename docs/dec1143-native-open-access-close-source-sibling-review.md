## Four operations are not equivalent

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

**The syscall timing matters.** `IN_OPEN` is a native open event; a read-only open need not yet have accessed content bytes. `IN_ACCESS` is a native file-access event from a real byte-read; it is not a content-write notification. `IN_CLOSE_NOWRITE` occurs on closing a read-side descriptor, potentially after separate actual read operations. An `O_PATH` path-only descriptor by itself does not emit those ordinary read-side notifications on the probed native Linux system. These distinctions require genuine Linux events and cannot be inferred reliably from a final byte digest.

**The watched inode and its parent provide different names.** An inode watch on `sample` yields an **unnamed** source IN_ACCESS when reading sample. A watch on the containing directory reports **named IN_ACCESS(public-sibling)** when reading an **unrelated** sibling inode, and no source-self event. These events are not interchangeable and cannot prove that the watched source bytes changed. A separate read witness is closed **before** test verification `read_bytes()` calls, since those otherwise produce extra true OPEN/ACCESS/CLOSE_NOWRITE events, contaminating expectations.

The original IN_ONESHOT subscribers are **intentionally unsafe test-only alternatives**. They expire on their first subscribed read-side event, reporting IN_IGNORED and then missing later genuine content mutations. The actual unchanged separately initialized helper `scripts/dec677_disposable_inotify_events.py::_start_watch` remains continuously subscribed to source+parent *mutation* events **without IN_ONESHOT or read-side OPEN/ACCESS/CLOSE_NOWRITE**. Subsequent actual changed-byte and restoring writes to sample are independently witnessed as **unnamed source IN_MODIFY/IN_CLOSE_WRITE and named parent IN_MODIFY/IN_CLOSE_WRITE(sample)**, even while original unsafe one-shot remains silent. Unchanged evaluator returns **status=BLOCKED**, **can_authorize_dispatch=false**, **independent_os_proof_verified=false**. Access time may change on reads depending on the mount; full metadata neutrality is not asserted.
