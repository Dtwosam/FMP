### Rank calibration identity

To avoid embedding potentially very large sorted calibration arrays, each active
feature persists:

- feature name;
- calibration value count;
- distinct-value count;
- minimum;
- maximum;
- SHA-256 of the canonical complete sorted calibration-value array.

Validation requires the active-feature order to match the calibration summaries,
at least 600 calibration values, at least 20 distinct values, finite range
endpoints, and valid calibration digests.

The exact calibration values are still deterministically generated by the pinned
DEC-453 miner from the pinned source artifacts. The evidence contract records their
identity without duplicating all values into the evidence artifact.

## Shortlist semantic validation

For every shortlisted hypothesis DEC-454 persists all eight annual 2015-2022:

- evaluable support;
- selected-tail support;
- signed rank slope at 0.5-pip cost;
- selected-tail mean at 0.5-pip cost;
- selected-tail mean at 1.0-pip stress.

The validator reconstructs `AnnualContinuousEffectStat`, re-runs the frozen
DEC-452 gate, recomputes every persisted metric, recomputes the hypothesis
fingerprint, and rebuilds the exact frozen rank key.

A re-fingerprinted metric tamper therefore still fails semantic validation.

Shortlist order must exactly match the frozen DEC-452 ranking.

Frozen fingerprints must equal the first up to two shortlist fingerprints.
