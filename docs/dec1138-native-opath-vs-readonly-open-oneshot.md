First, a genuine Linux `os.open(sample, O_PATH|O_CLOEXEC)` obtains a **path-only inode reference**, verified with `os.fstat`. While that descriptor is open and after it closes, both read-side inotify queues are **entirely quiet**, as is the normal mutation watcher. The test closes the read-event witness before any `sample.read_bytes()` verification to avoid verification-induced extra IN_OPEN/CLOSE_NOWRITE receipts.
