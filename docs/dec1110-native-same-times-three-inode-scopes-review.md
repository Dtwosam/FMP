**What is genuinely equal?** Each test asserts nanosecond `st_atime_ns` and `st_mtime_ns` immediately after both real utime calls equal the corresponding pre-call values; unchanged source/sibling PUBLIC file bytes and final dev/inode/mode are also checked. A same-timestamps utime may update ctime or other metadata. Thus, it must **not** be characterized as fully metadata-neutral, a source-content write, an OS-enforced no-write policy or a proof that all inode fields are equal. `IN_ATTRIB` is a genuine kernel notification but not itself evidence of changed bytes.
